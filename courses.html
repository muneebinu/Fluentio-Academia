Questions, answered.

Are the classes live or recorded?+
All classes are live on Zoom or Google Meet. Real speaking confidence comes from practising with a real instructor. Recordings are shared after each session.
Which German level should I start with?+
If you have never studied German, start with A1. If you already know the basics, message us on WhatsApp and we will help you choose the right level.
How long are the German courses?+
A1 is an 8-week intensive and A2 is a 10-week intensive. Message us for the B1 schedule.
I am a complete beginner in English. Is it suitable?+
Yes. The Standard plan is designed for beginners. We start from everyday spoken English and build up, with no heavy grammar theory.
What is the difference between Standard and Premium?+
Standard is group-based and ideal for general fluency. Premium adds 1-on-1 sessions, personal feedback, interview preparation and weekly progress tracking.
How do I pay?+
You can pay by EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer. Message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you through enrollment.
Do I receive a certificate?+
Yes. Certificates carry a QR code and can be verified on our website.
